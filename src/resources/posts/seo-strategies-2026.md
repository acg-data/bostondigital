Most SEO advice online is recycled from 2018. The strategies below are what we're actively using to rank Boston businesses on page one of Google in 2026 — based on real campaigns running right now.

## 1. Write for entities, not just keywords

Google's algorithm has shifted from matching keywords to understanding **entities** — people, places, things, and the relationships between them. When you write a page about "dental implants Boston," Google now looks for related entities: prices, procedures, recovery timelines, brand names, neighborhoods served, insurance providers.

**What to do:** When you write a page, ask yourself: "What does someone searching this term need to know?" Then cover every related entity comprehensively. A 1,500-word page that touches all the related entities will outrank a 3,000-word keyword-stuffed page every time.

## 2. Build topical authority through clusters

Single posts don't rank like they used to. What works in 2026 is **topic clusters**: one pillar page (broad topic) plus 8-15 supporting articles that link back to it.

Example: pillar page is "Boston SEO Guide." Supporting articles include "Boston Local Pack ranking factors," "Best Boston SEO agencies," "SEO pricing in Boston," etc. Each links back to the pillar with descriptive anchor text.

Google sees the cluster and says: this site is the authority on Boston SEO. The pillar page jumps up the rankings, dragging the cluster pages with it.

## 3. Show off your real-world expertise (E-E-A-T)

Google's E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) update gave a massive boost to sites that demonstrate real-world expertise. Generic AI-written content gets penalized.

**Concrete tactics:**

- Add author bylines with real bios + credentials on every post
- Include first-person stories and case study data ("we ran this campaign for X client and saw Y result")
- Cite primary sources, not just other blogs
- Get featured in industry publications and link those mentions on your About page

## 4. Optimize for AI search (SGE / ChatGPT / Perplexity)

A growing share of searches now happen in AI tools that summarize results. To get cited, structure your content like an answer:

- Lead with a **direct, short answer** in the first paragraph
- Use clear H2s as questions
- Add FAQs with structured data (schema.org/FAQPage)
- Include data tables and lists — AI summarizers love structured info

## 5. Local SEO basics still beat everything

For Boston businesses, the highest-ROI SEO work isn't fancy. It's:

- **Google Business Profile** fully filled out, posts weekly, reviews actively requested
- **Hyper-local landing pages** for each neighborhood you serve (Cambridge, Somerville, Newton, etc.)
- **NAP consistency** (Name, Address, Phone) across all directories
- **Local backlinks** from Boston organizations, chambers, sponsorships, and news

A boring, comprehensive local SEO foundation will outperform any clever content strategy if you serve a local market.

## The bottom line

2026 SEO isn't about gaming the algorithm. It's about being genuinely useful, technically clean, and locally relevant. The agencies winning right now are the ones doing all five above consistently, not the ones chasing the latest tactic.
