Email is unsexy. It's also, by a wide margin, the highest-ROI marketing channel for most local businesses — averaging $42 in revenue per $1 spent. The catch: it only works if you have automations running. One-off campaigns barely move the needle.

Here are the five automations every local service business should set up first.

## 1. The welcome sequence (the highest-ROI automation, period)

When someone gives you their email — through a contact form, lead magnet, or appointment booking — the first 7 days are critical. They just raised their hand. You have permission to talk. Use it.

**What we typically build:** A 5-email sequence over 14 days:

1. **Day 0** — Confirmation + what to expect
2. **Day 2** — Founder/team intro story (humanize the brand)
3. **Day 5** — Best educational content (your top-performing blog post or video)
4. **Day 9** — Social proof / case study
5. **Day 14** — Clear soft offer with calendar link

**Typical lift:** 20-35% of welcome sequence recipients become customers within 90 days.

## 2. Post-booking / pre-appointment

If you take appointments (med spa, dental, salon, contractor), the gap between booking and the appointment is the highest-anxiety period. Use it to reduce no-shows and prime them to spend.

**3-email sequence:**

1. **Booking confirmation** — clear date/time, what to bring, parking instructions
2. **48 hours before** — reminder + answers to common pre-appointment questions
3. **Morning of** — confirmation + add-on services they might want

**Typical lift:** 30-50% reduction in no-shows. 15-25% increase in average order value through add-on suggestions.

## 3. Post-purchase / post-service follow-up

The window 1-7 days after service is when customers are most likely to leave a review (good or bad) — and when they're most receptive to upsells and referrals.

**4-email sequence:**

1. **24 hours after** — "How did it go?" with a one-click feedback form
2. **3 days after** — Review request with direct links to Google + Yelp
3. **7 days after** — Referral offer ($X off when you refer a friend)
4. **30 days after** — "Time for your next [service]?" rebooking nudge

**Typical lift:** 4-8x more reviews. Referral rate increases 2-3x.

## 4. Win-back campaign for lapsed customers

You have a list of customers who haven't bought in 6+ months. Most agencies forget about them. Don't.

**3-email sequence triggered at 6 months inactive:**

1. **"We miss you"** — checking in, offering help
2. **"Here's what's new"** — what you've added since they last visited
3. **"Time-limited welcome-back offer"** — discount or freebie with a deadline

**Typical lift:** 8-15% of lapsed customers re-engage. Highest-margin revenue you'll ever generate.

## 5. Abandoned form / abandoned cart

If you have a contact form, multi-step quote tool, or e-commerce: track abandonment and follow up.

**For service businesses with multi-step quote forms:** if someone fills out 70% of a form but doesn't submit, send a follow-up 1 hour later. "Looks like you got partway through — anything we can help clarify?"

**Typical lift:** 12-25% recovery rate on otherwise-lost leads.

## How fast can this work?

In our experience, here's what happens 90 days after launching these five automations for a typical local service client:

| Metric | Before | After 90 days |
|---|---|---|
| Email-attributed revenue | 5% of total | 18-25% of total |
| Customer LTV | Baseline | +15-30% |
| No-show rate | 12-18% | 4-7% |
| Reviews per month | 2-5 | 15-30 |

That's not from cleverness or constant effort. That's from setting up these five automations once and letting them run.

For most local service businesses we work with, Mailchimp or ActiveCampaign is plenty.

## The takeaway

If you're spending money on ads or SEO but don't have email automation running, you're filling a bucket with a hole in it. The list you've already built is more valuable than the next lead you'll acquire — for cents on the dollar.
