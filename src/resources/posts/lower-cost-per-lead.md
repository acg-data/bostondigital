When a client comes to us with a high cost-per-lead problem, the first thing most agencies say is "you need to spend more." More budget = more data = better optimization, right?

Sometimes. But before we ever recommend more spend, we run four free audits that consistently cut CPA by 30-50% on existing accounts. Here's the framework.

## Audit 1: The conversion tracking sanity check

You'd be shocked how often we inherit accounts where conversion tracking is broken, double-counting, or misattributing. Common issues:

- **Multiple "Lead" conversion actions** firing on the same form submission
- **No conversion value** assigned (Google can't optimize for what it can't measure)
- **Wrong attribution model** (last-click in a multi-touch journey wastes 40%+ of spend on the wrong channels)

**The fix:** Run a "Conversions" diagnostics report in Google Ads. Pause any conversion action with the wrong category. Set a real lead value (even an estimate is better than zero). Move to a data-driven attribution model if you have 300+ conversions in 30 days.

**Typical impact:** 15-25% effective CPA reduction. You're not actually spending less — you're just no longer wasting it on bad signals.

## Audit 2: The match type purge

If you've inherited an old account, search terms are probably bleeding budget on irrelevant queries. The fix is brutal but works:

1. Pull the **Search Terms report** for the last 90 days
2. Sort by spend, descending
3. Flag every term that's not a buyer
4. Add as negative keywords (use **phrase or exact match** negatives, not broad)
5. Repeat for every high-spending campaign

In a recent home services account, this single audit eliminated $4,200/month of waste — 32% of total spend — and CPA dropped 38% without losing a single qualified lead.

## Audit 3: The ad creative refresh

Ads decay. The CTR on a winning ad will drop 30-50% over 6 months as your audience sees it repeatedly (ad fatigue). Higher CTR means lower CPC means lower CPA.

**Tactic:** Every 4-6 weeks, refresh your top campaigns with:

- New headlines (test angles, not just word swaps)
- New descriptions
- New imagery (for Display / Performance Max)
- New video creative variants (for YouTube and Demand Gen)

We typically see 15-30% CPA improvement in the 30 days after a creative refresh.

## Audit 4: The landing page conversion audit

You can have the cheapest clicks in the world, but if your landing page converts at 1.5% instead of 4%, your CPA is still 3x higher than it needs to be.

**Quick wins we check first:**

- Page load speed (every second above 2.5s costs you ~7% conversion)
- Above-the-fold clarity (does a visitor know what to do within 5 seconds?)
- Form length (cut every field that isn't strictly necessary)
- Mobile experience (most ad clicks are mobile — test on a real phone, not desktop)

A bad landing page is the most expensive thing you can have. A great one is the cheapest scaling lever you have.

## Putting it together

Run all four audits in sequence. We typically see the combined effect deliver:

| Audit | Typical CPA Reduction |
|---|---|
| Conversion tracking | 15-25% |
| Match types + negatives | 20-35% |
| Creative refresh | 15-30% |
| Landing page optimization | 25-50% |
| **Compound effect** | **30-50% lower CPA** |

The math compounds. A 25% reduction here plus a 20% reduction there plus a 15% on top doesn't add to 60% — it multiplies to roughly 50% lower CPA because each compounds on the previous improvement.
